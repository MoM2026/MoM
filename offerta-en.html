Frequently asked questions

What we get asked before signing.

Is Advisory mandatory after go-live?
No. It's strongly recommended — the platform's value depends on the discipline with which KPIs are read and turned into decisions — but it remains the client's choice. There is a "post-rollout without Advisory" path: MoM keeps managing the software subscription, while the company takes data governance in-house.
How is it different from "evolutive hours"?
Evolutive hours (item 04) are pay-as-you-go engineering: new dashboards, integrations, routing rules. Advisory (item 05) is governance and reading of the data. It's about "what happens on the engagement", not "what new things we build". Keeping them separate lets us always tell you what you're paying for.
How long does the adoption path take?
It depends on the perimeter and the starting maturity. The Diagnostic clarifies realistic timelines for the later phases: some clients reach progressive go-live within a few months, others prefer a more relaxed programme with verticals activated in waves. We don't sell a fixed calendar.
Can I move from Essentials to Standard?
Yes. Upgrades are agreed with notice and take effect from the next service period. Downgrades require closing the current quarter, so as not to interrupt a technical committee already under way.
What's inside an "operational audit"?
A sample check on a defined perimeter: adherence of executed preventive plans to the theoretical schedule, quality of closed WOs, completeness of the associated documentation, calibration of the IoT thresholds. The output is a signed audit note, with any corrective actions.
