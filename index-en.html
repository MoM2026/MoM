The method · three phases

An assessment first. Then a roadmap. Then a control room.

We don't sell software. We sell an engineered method backed by a platform — so you keep ownership of the journey, and we keep responsibility for the result.

PHASE 01

Facility Diagnostic Report

A 4-week independent audit. We score your FM maturity across six dimensions and hand back a paper report with twelve prioritised actions. Decide whether to continue at the end of the phase — no pressure.

~ 4 WEEKSINDEPENDENT
PHASE 02

Design & Roadmap

Six weeks to design the architecture of your control room: setup plan, vertical configuration, 12-month rollout calendar, acceptance KPIs. You approve the roadmap before any implementation begins.

~ 6 WEEKSAPPROVAL GATE
PHASE 03

Implementation & Roll-Out

Setup of CDE, BIM-FM, CMMS, IoT, EMS and the KPI dashboard. Configuration tailored to your asset register. Field teams trained. Progressive go-live across sites.

~ 22 WEEKSGO-LIVE
The platform · six verticals

One control room. Six verticals. Yours.

Built on standards, integrated with the systems you already use. We don't move your data — we put it where you can govern it.

01

Document Repository (CDE)

Single ISO 19650 archive: structure, versioning, approvals, traceability. The end of file shares and email attachments.

02

BIM-FM

Navigable 3D model of your assets, with COBie metadata. Click an asset, find every document and work order attached to it.

03

CMMS

Maintenance plans, work orders, teams, vendors, SLAs. Unified register replacing the spreadsheets and legacy CMMS islands.

04

IoT & Predictive

Your existing sensors fed into ML models. Dynamic thresholds replace fixed alarm thresholds. Anomalies become work orders.

05

Energy Management (EMS)

consolidated baseline, hourly granularity, sub-meter rollout. Real attribution of consumption — not just an aggregate bill.

06

KPI Dashboard

UNI 15341 indicators on a single screen. Monthly KPI report auto-compiled. The view the Direction actually opens.
